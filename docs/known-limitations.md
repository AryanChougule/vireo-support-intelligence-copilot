# Known Limitations

1. **Complaint themes are exploratory.** TF-IDF/K-Means clusters are useful for discovery but are not a validated support taxonomy.
2. **Repeat-contact is a proxy.** A second contact within seven days does not prove the first interaction failed or that the issue was identical.
3. **Modeled exposure is not realized savings.** The business goal uses the policy SLA-miss credit as an exposure model.
4. **Legacy timestamp anomalies remain visible.** The source rows are not edited or silently repaired.
5. **Gemini is not a database engine.** Natural-language answers are grounded in the structured analytical snapshot generated by Python, not arbitrary live SQL.
6. **LLM output still needs human review.** The deterministic layer prevents fabricated numerical inputs, but executive wording and recommendations should be reviewed before external use.
